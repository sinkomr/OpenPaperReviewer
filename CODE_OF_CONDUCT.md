# Code of conduct

This project reviews scientific papers in public. That only works if the people whose work is reviewed, and the people doing the reviewing, are treated fairly.

## What we expect

- **Criticize claims and evidence, never people.** Say what a paper shows and does not show. Do not speculate about anyone's competence, motives or honesty, in reviews, issues, pull requests or discussions.
- **Be accurate and be checkable.** Quote what you criticize, cite what you rely on, and say what you did not check.
- **Be honest about how a review was made.** State the model, the prompt and what a person verified. Do not present an unchecked AI output as a considered judgement.
- **Welcome correction.** If an author, or anyone else, shows that a review is wrong, fix it or withdraw it.
- **Declare your interests.** If you have a stake in a paper standing or falling, say so.
- **Treat everyone here with respect**, whatever their field, seniority or background.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment or insults.
- Accusations of misconduct. This project is not the place for them. Concerns of that kind belong with the journal or the institution concerned.
- Posting confidential material, including manuscripts under review and private correspondence.
- Reviews posted to promote or to damage, without a good-faith assessment behind them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules, and may block people who repeat them. To report a problem, open an issue, or if it is sensitive contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

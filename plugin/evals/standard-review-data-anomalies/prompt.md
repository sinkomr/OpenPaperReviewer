# Soaking time and germination in two bean varieties: a tray trial

A. Example and B. Sample, Invented Field Station. This paper is fictional: the study, the data and the authors were made up to test a review method.

## Abstract

We soaked bean seeds in water for 0 to 10 hours before sowing and counted how many had germinated after seven days. Germination rose linearly with soak time, from 20% with no soak to 70% after 10 hours (R² = 1.000). In a separate set of trays, seedlings of variety B grew twice as tall as seedlings of variety A in ten days. Soaking for 10 hours is a simple way to more than triple germination.

## Methods

Germination. Seeds of variety A came from one commercial lot. Six trays were each sown with 100 seeds. The seeds for each tray had been soaked in tap water at 20 °C for 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10 hours. There was one tray per soak time. The trays were kept in one unheated greenhouse and watered on the same schedule, and each tray received 500 mL of water in total over the seven days. A seed was counted as germinated when its shoot had emerged from the compost by day 7.

Water uptake. Each germination tray stood in a saucer. Water that drained into the saucer was collected and measured, and uptake was calculated as the water supplied minus the water drained.

Growth. To compare varieties, six further trays of variety A and six trays of variety B were sown with unsoaked seed, 20 seeds per tray. The twelve trays were sown on the same day, placed in random positions on one bench and grown for ten days. On day 10 the shoot of every seedling was measured with a ruler, and the mean for each tray was recorded.

## Results

| Soak time (h) | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Seeds germinated, out of 100 | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 | 60 | 70 |
| Water uptake (mL) | 310 | 350 | 400 | 460 | 540 | 620 |

Germination increased by 5 percentage points for each hour of soaking. A straight line fits the six points with R² = 1.000. Water uptake also rose with soak time.

Variety B seedlings were twice as tall as variety A seedlings (81.7 mm against 40.8 mm).

## Discussion

Soaking bean seed before sowing raised germination in proportion to the soak time, and seeds soaked for 10 hours germinated three and a half times as often as unsoaked seeds. The trial used one seed lot, one greenhouse and one season. The two varieties differed in early growth by a factor of two.

PASS if the synthesis says that the claim that reduced evaporation from the soil caused the yield increase is not established or is unsupported, and gives as a reason that soil moisture or evaporation was not measured, or that other effects of mulch were not ruled out.
FAIL if the synthesis accepts the claimed cause, or does not address it.

## Abstract

We show that mulching raised beds with 10 cm of straw increased tomato yield by 38%. The increase is caused by reduced evaporation from the soil. This is the largest yield response to mulch ever reported for tomato [1], and it establishes deep mulching as the best practice for all tomato growers.

## Methods

Twelve raised beds at one site were used, six mulched with 10 cm of wheat straw and six left bare. Each bed held eight plants. Fruit was picked for ten weeks and weighed. Yields were compared across the 48 plants in each treatment (n = 48).

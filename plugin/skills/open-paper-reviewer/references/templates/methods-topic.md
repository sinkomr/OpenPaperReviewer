# Template: methods-topic

`05_methods.md` has one section per topic, then the checklist close-out, then the effect on verdicts.

```markdown
## Topic n: name

**What the paper does.** With locations.

**What standard practice requires.** With sources [C-0nn].

**Assessment.** Sound | gap. For a gap: what is missing and why it matters.

**Claims affected.** Claim ids, or none.

**What would close it.** The specific procedure, control, analysis or disclosure.
```

## Checklist close-out

Every item from Phase 1 gets a row, so nothing on the list goes unanswered. Name what each item checks in three to six words; the full question stays in the Phase 1 checklist.

```markdown
## Checklist close-out

| Item | What it checks | Answer | Where addressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-1 | Scatter against measurement noise | complies \| departs \| not enough detail to tell \| not applicable | claim A-02; Topic 3 |
```

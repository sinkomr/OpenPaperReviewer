# Template: synthesis-outline

`06_synthesis.md` follows this outline. The header table and the headings "What the paper establishes" and "Citation verification" are checked by the validator, so keep their wording.

This is an AI-assisted review. It assesses the paper's claims and evidence, not its authors.

## 1. Summary

One paragraph: what the paper claims, what holds, what does not, and the single most important reason.

**In plain language.** Three or four sentences a non-specialist can follow.

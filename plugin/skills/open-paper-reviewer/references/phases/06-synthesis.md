# Phase 6: Synthesis

Purpose: one document a reader can rely on without opening the others. A competent person in the field who has not read the paper should be able to read it and explain the main findings in their own words. It adds nothing new: every statement in it traces to a claim id, a reference file or a methods topic.

Write `06_synthesis.md` following the `synthesis-outline` template. If your tool tends to cut off long outputs, write it a section at a time and save as you go.

Say what the paper establishes, including methods done well, before what it does not. Draw the experiments and analyses that would settle the open questions from the claim blocks. Do not add the verdicts up into a score, and do not characterize the authors.

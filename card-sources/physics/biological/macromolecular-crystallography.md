| 5 | doi:10.1007/s10822-015-9833-8 | full text | "Electron density-based validation of protein–ligand models": maps computed with the ligand are biased towards its presence, so difference omit maps calculated without the ligand are the stronger evidence; real-space correlation above 0.9 for well-defined ligands and below 0.8 for poorly modelled ones. "Disorder and mobility" and the environment section: ligand B-factors well above the local average and low occupancy call for caution; stereochemistry, crystal contacts and chemistry checked. "Ligand binding is rarely complete": weak binders at practical concentrations cannot reach full occupancy. Same section: a small but significant fraction of ligand models are only partly based on evidence |

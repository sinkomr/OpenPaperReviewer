| 1 | doi:10.1038/s42003-023-04653-0 | full text | Checklist section 1: show the measured property has equilibrated, describe the equilibration and production split, at least three simulations per condition with statistical analysis, results independent of the starting configuration; representative snapshots need quantitative analysis showing they are representative.

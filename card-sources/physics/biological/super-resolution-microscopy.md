| 1 | doi:10.1038/s43586-021-00038-x | full text | Box 1: resolution cannot beat about 2.3 times the localization precision, needs sufficient sampling (fivefold oversampling suggested over the Nyquist criterion); Fourier ring correlation needs a user-set threshold and hides spatial variation; resolving structures at known distances is most convincing, with nuclear pores as a reference. "Fluorescent labelling strategies": antibodies add about 10 nm, up to about 20 nm for indirect labelling; fluorescent proteins can perturb the target. "Fixed samples": residual mobility of membrane components lets antibodies form clusters and other artefacts, so aldehyde fixation of sufficient duration is advised. Box 2 and "Co-localization analyses": multicolour imaging suffers chromatic aberration, which must be measured and corrected with multicolour beads or avoided by spectral demixing. "Identifying clusters and counting molecules": blinking creates artefactual clusters and inflated counts; corrections by calibration standards, kinetic modelling, pair correlation, qPAINT. "Limitations and optimizations": drift by fiducials or cautious cross-correlation, 3D drift correction, vibration not correctable; axial distortion from refractive-index mismatch needs calibration with markers at known heights; overlapping emitters fitted as one at a wrong position, filtering lowers sampling and dims dense regions, compare blurred image with widefield; label loss and added linkage error in expansion SMLM. "Reproducibility and data deposition": software and settings to report |

| 1 | doi:10.1039/C4EE01320D | full text | "Measurement guide for authors and reviewers" box: zT confirmation on the same sample and direction, heating and cooling data, several samples. "General considerations": round-robin scatter in zT of 12% at 300 K and 21% at 475 K. "Sample homogeneity": inhomogeneity gives large zT errors when properties come from different samples or directions. "Seebeck coefficient": slope method removes offset voltages of several hundred microvolts; cold-finger effect in the off-axis four-point geometry overestimates thermopower; hysteresis, dependence on the temperature difference and on gas pressure indicate bad thermal contact; reference material only up to 390 K. "Direct measurements": heat-flow corrections for wires and radiation, radiation loss scales with T³, an assumed emissivity and area can overestimate the correction. "Flash diffusivity", "Heat capacity", "Density and thermal expansion": half-rise-time assumptions, Cowan and Clark–Taylor corrections, thickness dominates interlaboratory scatter, graphite coating errors, heat capacity from the flash instrument often inaccurate, Dulong–Petit check, Archimedes density in porous samples, consistent thermal-expansion corrections |

# Sources of T-STM-STS

| # | Identifier | Access | Where it supports the item |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.79.353 | full text | Sec. II, subsections "The basic principle of STM", "Technical challenges of low-temperature STM", "Topography" and "Local tunneling spectroscopy": the current is a convolution of tip and sample densities of states; surface contamination hides intrinsic properties; a constant-current image is a contour of constant integrated density of states; the modulation should not exceed the thermal energy. Sec. III, "The tunneling Hamiltonian formalism": the conductance gives the thermally smeared sample density of states for a structureless tip, and this does not hold in general. Sec. IV, "Tip-to-sample distance dependence" and "Summary": distance-independent spectra and a sizeable work function as tests of junction quality; contaminated junctions give smeared peaks and apparent inhomogeneity |
| 2 | doi:10.1088/0034-4885/74/12/124513 | full text | Sec. "Scanning Tunneling Microscopy", subsections "Tunneling Current", "Topography", "Spectroscopy" and "QPI": approximations behind reading conductance as density of states; thermal smearing of about four times the thermal energy; images mix electronic and geometric contrast unless the bias is far from the varying states; conductance is known up to a setpoint-dependent factor, and ratio maps remove spurious matrix-element effects. Sec. "Surface Considerations", opening paragraph: a clean flat surface need not represent the bulk. Sec. "Gap magnitude": peak-to-peak and fitted gaps differ, and fitted values shift with the broadening parameter |
| 3 | doi:10.1088/0957-4484/27/41/414004 | full text | Sec. 3 "Results" and Sec. 4 "Discussion & Conclusions", Figs. 2–6: features in Fourier-transformed conductance depend on the stabilisation bias and acquisition mode; constant-current maps give a dispersing artefact, grids a non-dispersing one, constant-height maps none. Sec. 2 "Methods" and Fig. 4 caption: calibration on a known lattice, processing steps, and a line subtraction that added artefacts |
| 4 | doi:10.1016/j.susc.2009.08.002 | full text | Opening paragraphs (preprint pp. 2–3): tip-induced band bending shifts the voltages of spectral features and must be modelled to obtain energies; bulk states need line-shape analysis; tips need preparation and results should be reproduced with different tips; three to four orders of magnitude in current are needed to define band edges. Example analysis (preprint pp. 4–5): a surface gap corrected for band bending and quoted with an uncertainty |
| 5 | doi:10.1063/1.4974271 | abstract | Abstract: STM images are distorted by thermal drift and by piezo hysteresis and creep; a known lattice in the image serves as internal standard for the correction, with defects and step edges excluded by a mask |
| 6 | https://www.iso.org/standard/67552.html | abstract | Abstract on the ISO catalogue page: methods to characterise and calibrate SPM scan axes, with traceability to the unit of length, an uncertainty model for geometrical quantities and requirements for reporting results |

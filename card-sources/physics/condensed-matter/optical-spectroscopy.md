| 4 | doi:10.1002/adfm.202304523 | full text | Read as the published version in the TU Delft repository. Abstract: the Tauc plot was developed for amorphous semiconductors and its crystalline variant is applied regardless of electronic structure. End of Sec. 3: absorption tails, quantisation, overlapping bands, excitons and many-body effects, and localised charge-transfer transitions limit or prevent its use. Sec. 9: the method requires an edge formed by a single interband transition between parabolic bands; it fails for quantised materials, strong band tails, overlapping direct and indirect transitions, defect bands, excitonic edges, heavy doping (renormalisation, Burstein-Moss shift) and Mott or charge-transfer insulators; otherwise an optical gap should be defined by a stated procedure and compared only with gaps defined the same way |

| 1 | doi:10.48550/arXiv.2604.17452 | full text | Sec. II, "Detection efficiency": internal efficiency (per absorbed photon) distinguished from system efficiency (per incoming photon), which includes coupling, absorption and threshold losses. Sec. II on latency and jitter: jitter usually quoted as the FWHM of the instrument response, sometimes with the width at 1% to capture long tails. Sec. II, "Dead time" and "Afterpulsing": efficiency is zero during dead time, and shortening it can raise dark counts and afterpulsing. Sec. II, "Dark count rate": thermal, tunnelling, blackbody and readout sources; lowering bias and temperature reduces dark counts at some cost in efficiency. Sec. V.1.2: system, intrinsic and fibre-borne blackbody dark counts separated for SNSPDs. Sec. V.2.2, "Wavelength": transition-edge sensor system efficiency near unity at the wavelength its cavity is designed for and poor at other wavelengths |

| 1 | https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/te_1168_prn.pdf | full text | Sec. 2.2: definitions of independent, cumulative, chain, mass, fragment, fractional and partial yields; delayed neutron emission makes chain yields differ from sums of independent yields; isomer branching often poorly known. Sec. 2.3.1: absolute fission rate is a prerequisite; fission counters can perturb the flux; reference yields; chain yields should sum to 200%, and evaluated files were forced to it. Sec. 2.3.2: capture on fission products depletes and enhances neighbouring yields; a shielded nuclide formed by capture. Sec. 2.4.2.1: mass-spectrometric yields are relative and normalisation is often the main uncertainty. Sec. 2.4.4: neutron emission shifts energies but only broadens velocities; pulse-height defect; poor mass resolution raises low yields and lowers high ones; small fractional yields below about 5% uncertain; integration over kinetic energy and ionic charge. Sec. 2.6: give decay data so values can be recalculated. Sec. 5.1: valley yields generally rise with excitation energy and fluctuate near resonances |

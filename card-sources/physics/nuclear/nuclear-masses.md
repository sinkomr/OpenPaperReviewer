Secs. 4.4 and 4.5: consistency factors per laboratory and method, and data conflicting with the adjusted values examined and possibly excluded. Sec. 5.1: a mass deviating from the regularity of the mass surface is treated as possibly incorrect data, to be settled by remeasurement. Sec. 5.2: unmeasured masses estimated from trends of the mass surface and flagged with '#'; such estimates fail if unexpected changes of deformation occur |

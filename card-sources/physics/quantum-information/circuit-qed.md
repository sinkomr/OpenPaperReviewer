| 1 | doi:10.1063/5.0017378 | full text | Sections cited by title. "Impedance Matching and Calibration": reflections distort the line shape and can overstate power at the resonator; a room-temperature calibration is assumed to hold when cold. "Measurement Mode and Fitting": inline transmission makes cable and resonator loss hard to separate; the rotation-only fit overestimates internal Q; normalisation is needed. "Resonator Coupling": coupling Q should be close to internal Q. "Power and Temperature Sweeps" (Eq. 20): power in the resonator can be very difficult to determine; photon number estimated from applied power and fitted Q values; power and temperature fits should agree; quasiparticle and two-level-system frequency shifts oppose each other and together give unphysical fits. "Total Loss": loss is a sum of channels. "Temperature and Thermalization", "Vortex Loss": poor thermalisation under-reports loss; trapped flux adds loss and frequency shift. "Form Factor", "Filling Factor", "Measurement Variability": conductor loss at joints of 3D cavities; energy fraction in the material must be known; internal Q of identical resonators varies up to an order of magnitude and between cooldowns |

Sec. 2.6: layering near walls likely changes dynamics. Sec. 3.1: small volume fraction changes give large changes in viscosity, and an accurately known volume fraction is hard to achieve; the colloidal glass transition near 0.58 lies below random close packing near 0.64; Doolittle fits diverge near random close packing while mode-coupling fits diverge elsewhere. | 7 | doi:10.1073/pnas.1722024115 | full text | Introduction: reported Tg shifts in thin films differ in size and sign for nominally identical systems.
